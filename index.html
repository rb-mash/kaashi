ABOUT US

PART OF KAASHI-O INVESTMENT CC

Located in the bustling commercial hub of northern Namibia, this cozy coffee spot is part of Kaashi-o Investment CC — a local business that originally specialized in printing services before expanding into hospitality. Our relaxed, modern interior is designed as an inviting community space, popular with digital nomads, local students, business travellers, and tourists alike.
